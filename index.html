Questions

When are you launching?

First in Lekki, Victoria Island and Ikoyi, with more of Lagos to follow shortly after.

How much does it cost?

You set your own task budget. E Assist takes a small platform fee from the assistant's side — there's no extra charge added on top of what you post.

Is my money safe?

Yes. Payment is held in escrow and only released once you confirm the task is complete, or resolved by our team if there's a dispute.

Can I do this part-time?

Yes — as an assistant, you choose which tasks to accept and when. There's no schedule to commit to.

Do I need to be verified to post a task?

No. Verification is required to complete tasks as an assistant, not to post one as a customer.
